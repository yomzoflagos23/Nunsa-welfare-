Quick guides

When exams and money get heavy

  • Tell one person you trust how you are doing. Saying it out loud lowers the weight.
  • Break the week into small tasks. One finished task counts as progress.
  • Sleep and a proper meal come before another late-night study session.
  • If your fees or feeding are the problem, report it. The office can often point you to help.

Stay safe online

  • No real scholarship, internship or loan asks you to pay a fee to receive it.
  • Never share an OTP, PIN or password, even with someone who says they are from your bank.
  • Check a link before you tap it. Fake portals copy real school pages.
  • Turn on screen lock and a tracking feature on your phone.
